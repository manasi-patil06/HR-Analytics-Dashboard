HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI

📊 Project Overview

This project is an HR Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee data and understand important workforce patterns. The dashboard presents employee-related information in an interactive and easy-to-understand format, helping users explore areas such as employee attrition, demographics, job roles, salary, experience, job satisfaction, performance, and work environment.

The project consists of two interactive dashboard pages, with each page focusing on a different aspect of employee and organizational analysis. The dashboard uses Power BI visuals, filters, KPI cards, and interactive data exploration to convert raw employee data into meaningful insights.

🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze employee demographics and workforce characteristics.
- Understand employee attrition patterns.
- Compare attrition across different job roles, departments, age groups, and other categories.
- Analyze salary, experience, and employee tenure.
- Explore employee satisfaction and work-life balance.
- Understand relationships between job satisfaction, environment satisfaction, training, and performance.
- Present HR data through an interactive and visually organized dashboard.

📄 Dashboard Pages

Page 1 – Employee & Attrition Analysis

The first page focuses on understanding the overall employee workforce and attrition patterns. It provides an overview of employees based on factors such as age, gender, education, department, job role, job level, salary, and years of experience.

The page also explores employee attrition across different categories, making it possible to identify how workforce characteristics vary between employees who stayed with the organization and those who left.

Interactive filters allow users to analyze the data according to different employee categories and examine specific segments of the workforce.

Page 2 – Employee Satisfaction & Performance Analysis

The second page focuses on employee experience, satisfaction, and performance. It explores factors such as job satisfaction, environment satisfaction, work-life balance, training, performance ratings, job roles, and employee levels.

The page helps in understanding how employee satisfaction and workplace-related factors vary across different groups. Interactive visuals allow users to compare these factors and examine the data from different perspectives.
